## Aggregation

- Median Modal_Price per market-week (collapses variety/grade mixing): 53 weeks x 426 markets

## Outliers

- Winsorised per market at Tukey fences (k=3.0): 156 weekly values clipped (clipped, not deleted)

## Missing values

- Time-interpolated gaps up to 4 weeks inside each series: 361 of 1097 missing weeks filled; rest left NaN (nan-aware features)

## Feature engineering

- 15 candidate features (State and coverage excluded from clustering): mean_modal = Mean weekly modal price (Rs/quintal); median_modal = Median weekly modal price; min_price = Lowest weekly modal price; max_price = Highest weekly modal price; std_modal = Std of weekly modal price; cv = Coefficient of variation = std / mean; range_ratio = (max - min) / median of weekly modal price; mean_spread = Mean daily (Max-Min)/Modal intraday spread; skewness = Skewness of weekly price distribution; kurtosis = Excess kurtosis of weekly price distribution; seasonal_amp = (max - min) of calendar-month mean price / overall mean; trend_slope = Linear trend per year / mean price (relative drift); volatility = Mean absolute week-on-week % return; share_above_regional = Share of weeks priced above the cross-market weekly median; avg_arrivals = Mean reported daily arrivals (supply volume)

## Encoding

- State is kept only as a post-hoc interpretation label; no one-hot encoding was needed because every clustering feature is numeric.

## Correlation pruning

- Dropped 'std_modal' (|r|=0.991 with 'cv')
- Dropped 'mean_modal' (|r|=0.977 with 'median_modal')
- Dropped 'cv' (|r|=0.948 with 'seasonal_amp')
- Dropped 'range_ratio' (|r|=0.912 with 'seasonal_amp')

## Missing values

- Feature-level median imputation applied to 6 remaining NaN cells

## Scaling

- StandardScaler fitted once on 426 markets x 11 features (unsupervised, so no label leakage); mean~0, std~1 asserted

## Model selection

- Chosen: manhattan K=3; runner-up manhattan K=4
